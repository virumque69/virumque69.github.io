Left Hand Exercises

Slurs/Legato/Hammer-ons/Pull-offs

These develop hand position, finger strength and independence, coordination and dexterity.

Key: numbers grouped together indicate fingers. The first is sounded by striking the note with the right hand, subsequent notes are produced by hammering on (next number is greater than previous number) or pulling off (next number is less than previous number)

Examples:
01 - play the open string and hammer-on with the first finger
12 - play a note with the first finger and hammer-on with the second
121 - play a note with the first finger, hammer on with the second, and then pull off back to the first, without re-striking the string.

Walkthrough of 0123432101234.......:
Play the open string
Hammer on with the first finger
Hammer on with the second finger. Leave the first finger where it is.
Hammer on with the third finger. Leave the first and second fingers where they are.
Hammer on with the fourth finger. Now you have all four fingers down in four frets.
Pull off with the fourth finger without moving the other three. The pull off should be a "rest stroke" - the fourth finger pulls straight down and stops against the next string (unless you are on the first string). Leave the fourth finger resting against the nest string.
Pull off with the third finger, doing a "rest stroke". Now you have fingers one and two on the original string, and fingers three and four resting against the next string.
Pull off with the second finger likewise.
Pull off with the first finger. Now you have all four fingers resting on the next string.
Hammer on with the first finger back to the original string, without moving the other three fingers from their resting position.
Hammer on with the second finger.
Continue as above.
(This particular way of practicing is suited to this one exercise and need not be applied to all combinations)

"Spider" Exercises

Place all four fingers on any four consecutive frets on the sixth string.
Without playing anything, move your first finger to the fifth string. Do not move the other three fingers.
Leaving the first finger on the fifth string and the third and fourth fingers on the sixth string, move the second finger to the fifth string.
Move the third finger to the fifth string.
Move the fourth.
Now you have all four fingers on the fifth string. Move the first finger to the fourth string, without disturbing the other fingers, etc...

Make up other combinations:

Place all four fingers.
Lift one and three simultaneously without moving two and four.
Move one and three over the fifth string.
Place one and three on the fifth string.
Lift two and four without moving one and three, etc...
