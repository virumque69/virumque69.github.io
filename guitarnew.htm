Welcome to my guitar page.

I am a musician living in New York City. My training is classical, I have a M.M. degree from the Juilliard school, which I received in 1991. I was the first guitarist to give a Master's Degree graduation recital at Juilliard, where I studied with Sharon Isbin. I received my B.M. from Mannes College of Music in 1981, where I studied with Leonid Bolotine. I have also studied with or taken master classes with Oscar Ghiglia, Eliot Fisk, Lionel Party, Jose Maria Moreno (flamenco) and Hubert Howe (electronic and computer music).  I now teach a class in the evening division at Juilliard, called "Developing Classical Guitar Techinque".

I am also a composer and improvisor, and you can hear some of my music and access scores. I also play Just Intonation guitar.

I am available for performances and lessons. Please feel free to email me with any questions.
