IN THIS HOUSE

In this house
there are meetings of men.
These men meet to consider
the Logos.
The world is not interested
in these men's considerations,
the world has no time
for the Logos.
Nevertheless,
the meetings continue,
and the men consider
both the Logos and the world.
The presence of this house
in the world
is the presence of the Logos,
and the words spoken by these men
are words of the Logos.
This house is not a church,
nor are the men priests or ministers.
Nevertheless,
the meetings continue,
and the Logos and the world
are considered by men
in this house.
