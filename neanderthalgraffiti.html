Neanderthal Graffiti

It was the
androgynous foreigner
who nearly erased
the dervish's erotic
thaumaturgy.

Each derrick
thaws.
Every andiron
erubesces.

By dinner,
old timer,
easily derogated
lawyers had
been hammered
by dermal eruptions.

Thatch eaves
deracinate the
Easter earth.

After neap tide,
amidst the
Nearctic thaw,
eager newcomers
sought to derive
easy thalers
by pandering to
thanatoid ear-wigs.

Erratum:
Murder by derringer
of derelict androids.
